1. WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------
A single-purpose website that feels like reading a very old,
treasured book. The book is "The Kumar Method": ten chapters of
plain rules about money and life, written in the voice of a
Chartered Accountant who rebuilt his life across three countries
(India -> Bahrain 1990 -> America 1995; wife Sheba). The chapters
cover: the Basics, Financial Discipline, Family & Money, Friends &
Money, Investing, Betting & Predicting, Starting Your Own Company,
Moving Countries, Time Being Worth More Than Money, and Legacy.

The experience: a cinematic 3D launch intro (the physical book
spins out of darkness, its gold gilding ignites, the camera dives
into the cover) that hands off into a page-by-page reading view of
aged paper. 110 pages total: each chapter is 1 opener page (title +
italic-voiced intro with drop caps, styled after the owner's print
mockup) + 10 pages of one rule each.

The spine of the physical book carries a "ramp" logo; the ramp
logo also appears as a bronze sign-off inside the golden flash at
the end of the intro. This appears to be a Ramp (fintech) branded
artifact — treat the logo as intentional.

2. THE OWNER'S TASTE — HARD RULES, DO NOT VIOLATE
----------------------------------------------------------------
These were each learned from direct feedback. Breaking them will
get the work rejected:

- REALISM OVER FLASH. Anything that reads as a "web effect" gets
  called cheesy. The fade+drift page turn was rejected. Heavy
  deckled page edges were rejected ("in between vintage and UI").
  Fake-feeling background circles were rejected.
- Single-page turns are INSTANT. No animation. (Exception: the
  two-page spread mode uses a fast 3D leaf flip around the spine —
  740ms, cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.04, 0.14, 1) — because it mimics a
  real book leaf. That was requested: "really fast and really
  majestic.")
- SPARKLE LEVEL IS 10%. The gilding ignition was reduced twice
  (100% -> 75% -> 10%). Current: emissiveIntensity multipliers 0.3
  front / 0.26 spine, bloom 0.12 + ignite*0.08 + dive*0.9. Do not
  raise without being asked.
- NO Contents menu / ribbon. Navigation is only: click zones
  (left third back on single page, halves on spread), arrow keys,
  space, swipe.
- NO page tilt/rotation between pages.
- NO glow behind the book in the READING view. (The intro's
  build-and-detonate glow is separate and was requested.)
- Page edges: nearly straight, long gentle waves, low-frequency.
  High-frequency edge fuzz reads as "stubble" on mobile — rejected.
- Page corners are SQUARE — decided 2026-08-10, the A/B toggle was
  removed. Do not reintroduce rounded/worn corners.
- EVERY risky/aesthetic change goes in ITS OWN COMMIT with a clear
  message, so the owner can say "revert that" and it's one
  git revert + redeploy. This is a standing contract. Honor it.
- The owner dictates by voice; messages arrive mid-work and may
  contain transcription noise ("she" for "you", etc.). Read intent.
- User's other conventions (from their global memory): ⌥Space is
  taken by Superwhisper; they browse in Comet + Safari; never
  launch Dia; ask before headless browser automation.

4. THE 3D INTRO — HOW THE BOOK IS BUILT (Intro3D.tsx)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The book is NOT an AI-generated mesh and NOT a video. It is a real
3D object constructed at runtime in three.js (react-three-fiber):

- GEOMETRY: four boxes — front cover, back cover, a spine hub that
  sits slightly proud like real binding, and an inset page block.
  Proportions are MEASURED FROM THE SCANS: H=1.65, W=H*(700/1088)
  (front scan aspect), D=H*(230/852)*0.9 (spine scan aspect).
- TEXTURES: the owner's actual cover scans are mapped onto the
  outer faces. The same images double as bumpMap (embossing
  relief) and roughnessMap. Page block edges use a procedural
  gilded-stripe canvas texture.
- WINE TINT: covers are recolored per-pixel at load (useWineTint):
  non-gold pixels shift toward deep burgundy (r*0.66, g*0.52,
  b*1.45+12); gold pixels are detected and left warm. Original
  scans on disk are untouched — the tint is runtime-only.
- GOLD MASK (useGoldMask): the front + spine scans are scanned
  pixel-by-pixel (gold = r>128 && g>84 && g>r*0.5 && b<g*0.92 &&
  r+g>236, then 1.2px feather blur) to build an emissiveMap, so
  ONLY the gilded emblems/lettering ignite. Threshold is strict on
  purpose: looser values make leather pixels "sparkle" (rejected).
- TIMELINE (5.3s, driven in useFrame; clock zeroes on the FIRST
  RENDERED FRAME so slow texture loads don't eat the opening):
    0.0–3.3s  spin: rotY from 2.7π to 0, easeOutQuart; float bob;
              scale 0.84 -> 1.
    2.5–3.5s  ignition: emissive + bloom ramp (at 10% level), with
              a subtle sin() flicker like candlelight.
    3.85–5.05s dive: easeInExpo dolly from z=4.15 to z=0.43, FOV
              widens 40->55, slight camera roll. The dolly STOPS
              before the scan runs out of pixels; the last stretch
              is carried by a DOM gaussian rack-focus blur (16px
              desktop / 10px mobile) + animated film grain overlay.
    ~4.8–5.2s dissolve into GOLDEN light (not black), ramp logo in
              bronze; Experience then shows the reader under a
              1.1s handoffFlash fade-out. Skip = click or key
              (Enter/Esc/Space) — skipping cuts straight to the
              reader with NO flash.
- ATMOSPHERE: .introGlow, a blurred amber DOM layer behind the
  canvas, breathes with the ignition then DETONATES mid-dive
  (opacity/scale driven per-frame via onAtmos; burst = d*(1-d)*4).
- POST: Bloom (mipmapBlur, radius 0.42, threshold 0.55) — radius
  kept tight because a wide halo read as a fake "growing circle"
  (rejected). EffectComposer multisampling: 4 desktop, 0 on coarse
  pointers.
- MOBILE: dpr capped [1,1.5], gl antialias off (composer covers
  it), cheaper blur. Textures preload at module scope.

5. THE READER (Book.tsx + globals.css)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- LAYOUT: page proportions fixed (aspect 0.66), sized by container
  queries — ALL page typography is in cqw units relative to each
  leaf's own container (.leafBox has container-type: size). Fonts:
  EB Garamond (body, old-style numerals via "onum"), IM Fell
  English (drop caps, rule numbers, eyebrows), IM Fell English SC
  (running heads). Loaded via next/font.
- PAPER STACK (bottom to top inside .paper): tone gradients ->
  photoPaper (the real scan, multiply-blended, saturate(0.5)
  brightness(1.22) contrast(0.9), opacity 0.45, and PER-LEAF
  sampling: seeded mirror/pan/zoom so no two pages share the same
  creases) -> mirrored ghost show-through of the NEXT page ->
  seeded foxing stains + edge smudge -> content -> SVG grain
  (0.15) -> large blotch (0.08) -> edgeShade (binding-side
  darkening + worn corners). Page silhouette is cut by an SVG
  turbulence-displaced mask (low frequency 0.006/0.0045, 2
  octaves, scale 4–5; rx=12 worn variant, rx=0 square variant).
- SPREAD MODE: media query (min-width 900px + landscape) shows two
  facing pages; navigation moves 2; verso/recto edge shading is
  mirrored; folios sit on outer corners; a soft gradient spine
  runs down the center; page turns use the 3D leaf flip (§2).
  Portrait/phone = single page, instant turns.
- The chapter opener auto-shrinks type for long intros (dense /
  denser classes) and auto-fits the big title to its longest line.

8. KNOWN TRAPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Turning reactStrictMode back on will crash the intro's
  EffectComposer in dev (WebGL context churn). Leave it off or
  re-test thoroughly.
- Don't put `emissiveMap: undefined` in material constructor
  params — three.js warns; assign conditionally after creation.
- All page randomness must stay seeded (SSR/client hydration).
- The gold-mask threshold and the sparkle multipliers are TASTE
  CONSTANTS, not free parameters.
- `vercel logs <url>` follows/streams — it will hang a foreground
  shell; run with a timeout or in background.
- The repo path contains a space ("app danger") — always quote.
- NEVER apply a filter-heavy SVG data-URI (feTurbulence/
  feDisplacementMap) directly as a CSS mask-image on the page:
  Chromium intermittently fails to rasterize it and the ENTIRE page
  paints invisible. The edge masks are rasterized to PNG at runtime
  in Book.tsx (rasterizeMask -> CSS vars --paper-mask-worn/-square)
  with fallback none, so a mask failure can only mean straight
  edges, never a missing page.
- The "stars" in the intro are ON the book (soft points drawn into
  the emissive gold mask from the leather's natural glints), NOT
  floating room particles — the owner explicitly rejected drei
  Sparkles fields twice. Don't re-add them.

GOLD MASK RECALIBRATION PROTOCOL (mandatory on every book swap):
The ignition glow is driven by buildGoldMask() in Intro3D.tsx, whose
color thresholds are TUNED TO THE CURRENT TEXTURES. A new book.glb
means new tones and the mask WILL silently miss (red-leather book =
yellow-gold rule; plum book = copper/warmth rule keyed on r-b). Any
time public/book.glb changes:
 1. Extract textures: npx @gltf-transform/cli copy book.glb out.gltf
 2. Replicate the mask pipeline in Python (PIL) against the real
    baseColor atlas and RENDER A PREVIEW IMAGE — look at it. Target:
    title/emblem/border/logo coverage ~8-10% of atlas, leather field
    completely clean, stars scattered evenly.
 3. Only then port the tuned constants into buildGoldMask() (both the
    gilding rule and the star-glint floors) and ship.
Never assume the old thresholds transfer. Never ship without seeing
the mask preview.
